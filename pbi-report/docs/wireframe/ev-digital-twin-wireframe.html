PPO undercuts the £0.45 peak rate all day and monetises hours the clock gives away
Why this beats a clock
The ToU schedule commits to one price per window, whatever the queue looks like.
PPO's hourly means sit between the tariff levels (e.g. £0.353 at 08:00): on some days it charges high, on others low, conditioned on state — a behaviour no fixed schedule can express.
Compare the arrivals profile on page 1: the price ramp tracks the observed demand ramp, then releases capacity after the afternoon peak.
Mean across evaluation episodes; per-episode prices vary around these means.
